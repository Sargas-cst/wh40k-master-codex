# Pariah Nexus & the 4th Tyrannic War

## The Contra-Empyric Nexus {#nexus}

The **Pariah Nexus** — known to the Necrons themselves as the **Contra-Empyric Nexus** — is a Necron
pylon array in the Nephilim Sector, within Ultima Segmentum.[^lex-pariah-nexus] Also called the
**Zone of Silence** and the **Nephilim Anomaly**, it is **a region of space that seems to be free
from the influences of the Warp**.[^lex-pariah-nexus]

The Imperium's name for it records an inference rather than an understanding: **the Imperium thus
named it after the Pariah Gene, which has a similar effect of suppressing the
Warp**.[^lex-pariah-nexus] That gene is the blank of [[I.T2.Ch6]] — a human so inert in the Warp as
to exert negative psychic influence. The Imperium met a region where the Warp does not reach and
named it after the only comparable thing it knew.

What it actually is, is the largest engineering project in the setting's present. **In truth, the
Nexus is a vast Blackstone array constructed by the Necrons — more specifically the Cryptek Szeras and
the Nihilakh Dynasty. Considered the pinnacle of Necron cosmic engineering, at the heart of the
network is the Xendu System, where an immense Blackstone cage has been
constructed.**[^lex-pariah-nexus]

**Szeras** is the name to stop on. [[III.T1.Ch2]] records him overseeing the biofurnaces of
biotransference — the machinery that consumed the Necrontyr's flesh while the C'tan drank the
discarded souls. The Cryptek who industrialised his own species' conversion is now building the
galaxy's largest anti-Warp structure.

Its architecture is described in terms that concede the Imperium cannot read it. Around the Xendu
System lie **vast amounts of Necron orbitals and warships to guard over their great work**, and
further out **networks of vast Blackstone pylons, their deployment extending through patterns of
non-euclidean fractal crypto-logic incomprehensible to mortal minds**. Each pylon is **a unique
colossal structure whose purpose was to sustain and extend a field of anti-empyric
energy**[^lex-pariah-nexus] — and they were **seeded on target worlds with an immense Pylon
Ship**.[^lex-pariah-nexus]

Set that beside [[III.T4.Ch20]] and the symmetry is exact. For ten thousand years Abaddon destroyed
pylons to thin the barrier between realspace and the Warp; the Necrons are now manufacturing and
seeding them at industrial scale to thicken it. [[I.T1.Ch4]] records that noctilith works both ways.
Two powers are operating the same technology in opposite directions, and the Imperium — whose
[[II.T3.Ch8]] tech-priests could not scan a pylon — is in the middle.

The Imperium's own encounter with the field is recorded as a medical problem. Imperials in the region
**soon found themselves affected by a damaging malaise and energy-sapping field dubbed "The
Stilling"**, and **it is speculated the Stilling originates from the many pylons in the
Nexus**.[^lex-pariah-nexus]

*Speculated.* The Imperium is fighting inside a structure that is draining its soldiers and has not
established why.

## Three Wars in One Place {#three-wars}

**During the Indomitus Crusade, the Imperial Battlegroup Kallides was sent to investigate the Nexus,
instigating a war there.**[^lex-pariah-nexus]

What that war became is the most crowded conflict in the codex. **The ensuing war within the Pariah
Nexus saw not only the Imperium under Belisarius Cawl's Battle Group Hephaestus battle the forces of
the Silent King, but even a Necron civil war between the forces of Szarekh and Imotekh. Recently,
Chaos forces under Vashtorr have arrived in the area.**[^lex-pariah-nexus]

Three separate wars, overlapping. Take them in turn, because each closes a thread from earlier in this
Volume.

**Cawl is there.** The Archmagos of [[III.T5.Ch24]] who built the Primaris from STC technologies, and
who in [[III.T5.Ch25]] stabilised the Attilan Gate using Necron pylon data from a derelict World
Engine, has brought a battle group to the largest pylon array in existence. Whatever the Imperium
says its objective is, the man leading its scientific effort has a demonstrated interest in this
technology.

**Szarekh is there, and he is being fought by his own people.** [[III.T4.Ch21]] records the Silent
King ending a sixty-million-year exile in 744.M41 because he had met the Tyranids in the intergalactic
void, and beginning to reawaken Tomb Worlds so his species could unite against them. It also records
Imotekh the Stormlord as one of the leaders doing the uniting. They are now at war with each other
inside the Nexus. The division that [[III.T4.Ch21]] identified as the only thing preventing a Necron
force numerous enough to match humanity has not healed; it has turned into a civil war fought over
the species' greatest work.

**And Vashtorr has arrived.** The same Chaos power that launched the Arks of Omen Campaign with
Abaddon in [[III.T5.Ch25]] — the campaign that destroyed or corrupted an entire Indomitus Crusade
fleet. A structure designed to exclude the Warp has attracted the attention of the Warp.

## Two Tendrils {#tendrils}

While all of that proceeded, the thing the Silent King came back for arrived.

**The Fourth Tyrannic War began at some point in the Era Indomitus with the largest invasion of
Leviathan yet, when it attacked the Western Reaches of Segmentum
Pacificus.**[^lex-leviathan]

The assault's design is what distinguishes it. **The assault began with the coordinated attack from
above and below the galactic plane by two new tendrils, dubbed Nautilon and Promethor
respectively.**[^lex-leviathan] Nautilon moved **into the Vynor Sector from above the galactic plane**
while Promethor moved **into the Morpheum Sector from below it** — and **these two tendrils were
coordinating with one another in a roughly paralleled eastern
advance**.[^lex-leviathan]

Compare Behemoth in [[III.T4.Ch21]], which the sources describe as showing **no subtlety in its
berserker advance**. Leviathan's newest offensive is a two-pronged pincer entering the galaxy from
outside the plane on both sides at once, moving in parallel. The Hive Mind of [[VI.T4.Ch13]] has spent
two and a half centuries learning, and this is what it learned.

The destination is stated plainly. **It was a route that, if unaltered, would take them into Segmentum
Solar and towards Holy Terra itself.**[^lex-leviathan]

And the timing was not accidental in effect, whatever the Hive Mind intends. **With the Imperium
distracted by the Indomitus Crusade, few forces initially stood against
it.**[^lex-leviathan] [[III.T5.Ch25]] records the Crusade being manned by stripping garrisons from
systems judged untenable, over the objections of soldiers who mutinied rather than leave. The Fourth
Tyrannic War entered a Segmentum whose defenders had been redeployed.

## How the Imperium Found Out {#discovery}

The discovery sequence is the third time in two Titles that the Imperium receives decisive
intelligence and mislays it, and by now the pattern is the finding.

**The first indication of the Tyranid threat was silence across much of Segmentum Pacificus as the
Shadow in the Warp descended. The loss of communication with many outlying systems initially went
unnoticed by several Sector commands due to the typically slow wheels of Imperial bureaucracy, the
presence of Warp Storms, as well as simultaneous Ork and Knydd
threats.**[^lex-leviathan]

Whole systems went quiet and nobody noticed, because the paperwork is slow, the Warp is bad, and there
were other wars. [[III.T4.Ch21]] recorded the Explorator census of dead worlds around Tyran being
filed and ignored a decade before Behemoth; this is the same institutional failure at Segmentum
scale.

What broke through was a death. **The scale of the threat was finally discovered in the Morpheum
Sector command thanks to the horrific death of High Astropath Guideoth. Consumed by the Shadow in the
Warp, the Astropath was transformed into a tendrilled monstrosity that wrought destruction across the
Astropathic Choir before finally being put down.**[^lex-leviathan]

The Imperium learned it was being invaded because one of its telepaths turned into a Tyranid organism
inside a choir chamber on Terra's own network. That is the Astra Telepathica of [[IV.T9.Ch29]]
functioning as an early-warning system by being eaten.

Then the reports arrived at once, and the sources give three vignettes worth keeping. **The Byroth
Sub-sector was consumed by Tyranids as its Imperial Guard forces battled Orks.** At the **Trinary Reach
Cluster, a Leagues of Votann fleet suddenly retreated as the Imperial defenders of several factory
worlds rejoiced — only for the Tyranids to descend in their wake.** And **vast refugee fleets began
arriving** in the Haratus Stars and elsewhere.[^lex-leviathan]

The Votann episode is the sharpest of the three. The Kin of [[III.T2.Ch7]] — the branch of humanity
that kept its STCs and improves on them — withdrew because they had detected what was coming. The
Imperium celebrated their retreat as a victory, and then met the Tyranids.

The response was what the rest of this Volume would predict. **The Imperial response to this horror
was typically inconsistent, with some Governors overseeing total mobilization to conduct a defence
while others simply led a mass abandonment. Some Space Marine and Imperial Navy elements sought to
wage whatever defence they could while other Astra Militarum armies began to attempt to create
defensive lines to stem the Xenos advance.**[^lex-leviathan]

That is where Volume III ends, and it ends without resolution, which is honest. The Imperium is split
in two by [[III.T5.Ch23]]'s Rift. Its reconquest has lost a fleet to a daemon primarch. Its greatest
technologist is inside a Necron megastructure that is draining his soldiers for reasons nobody has
established, alongside a Necron civil war and an arriving Chaos power. Two coordinated Tyranid
tendrils are advancing on Terra from above and below the galactic plane through a Segmentum whose
garrisons were withdrawn. And the being on the Golden Throne has, per [[III.T3.Ch17]], a few centuries
of function left, bought with billions of lives.

Sixty million years earlier, in [[III.T1.Ch5]], the Silent King looked at a galaxy he had won and
concluded that the time of all flesh would pass. He is currently fighting his own species inside the
machine built to keep the Warp out, having come home because something worse than either was on its
way. Nothing in this Volume suggests he was wrong.
