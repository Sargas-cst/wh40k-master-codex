# Space Marine Anatomy (The Implants)

## What Is Recruited, and From Where {#recruitment}

A Space Marine is a manufactured object, and this Chapter is the specification. Before the
organs, the intake — because what the Imperium selects for is not what a reader expects.

**Recruits are chosen from the best warriors among humanity.** **Feral Worlds** are **prized
recruitment grounds, as such harsh and primal conditions produce the best warriors**. But the
preference is stated plainly: **Hive Worlds are considered the ideal source of potential
recruits, the populace of the lower levels composed of some of the most murderous scum in the
human Imperium.**[^lex-space-marines]

The lower levels of [[IV.T2.Ch4]] — where the heat sink delivers warmth and no power, where
the undercity recycles air and people. The Imperium's finest soldiers are drawn from the part
of its cities it has most thoroughly abandoned. And the collection method is not a
recruitment drive: **whole gangs of hive scum are sometimes hunted down and captured for
recruitment.**[^lex-space-marines]

*Hunted down and captured.* The masterless people of [[IV.T2.Ch4]] — the deserters, fallen
nobles and gangers the Imperium files under one word — are taken.

What is being selected for is stated without euphemism. **Among the most valued traits in a
recruit are aggression and psychotic-level killer instinct.**[^lex-space-marines] More rarely,
**certain Civilised Worlds are also recruited from**.[^lex-space-marines]

Then the filter. **The potential recruit is first subjected to testing, including tissue
compatibility tests and psychological screening. Relatively few get past this initial
selection process. Those that do pass are termed "neophytes", and the process continues with
the surgery, indoctrination, conditioning, and training that will make them
Marines.**[^lex-space-marines]

Ages matter, and they are young. The first implants are **ideally between 10 and 12 years of
age**, and the sequence runs through the recruit's teens, with **hypnotherapy normally
beginning at phase 6, ideally sometime between 14 and 17**.[^lex-creation-of-a-space-marine]

Ten years old. [[IV.T2.Ch6]] records the Schola Progenium sorting its children into careers at
about the same age, and the Officio Assassinorum of [[IV.T1.Ch3]] taking its candidates
younger still. Every elite institution in this Volume acquires its people before they can
consent, and the Astartes acquire them from the bottom of the hive.

What results is worth stating before the mechanism. Space Marines **are larger, stronger,
faster, and more durable than average humans**, with **higher cognitive abilities, such as
very accurate powers of recollection and a capacity to process information within mere
nanoseconds**.[^lex-space-marines] Fully armoured, one **typically stands 2.1 metres (6'8") to
3.0 metres (9'10") tall and weighs 500–1,000
kilograms**.[^lex-space-marines]

Two consequences of the design are less often stated. **Due to the nature of the zygotes that
make up Astartes gene-seed, all Space Marines are male.**[^lex-space-marines] And: **these
humans transformed for the sole purpose of war are rendered sterile by the Emperor's design.
Much like the Custodes, they are mentally tempered to have no sexual drive or desire to have
children and reproduce. Instead, these urges are rerouted through their psycho-indoctrination
towards aggression and the pursuit of goals.**[^lex-space-marines]

Sterility by design, and desire redirected into aggression by conditioning. The Emperor did
not merely build soldiers; he built soldiers who could not found dynasties. Set that beside
[[III.T3.Ch12]], where twenty Primarchs were engineered and half of them defected, and the
precaution reads as a lesson learned in advance and applied to the rank and file.

How long they last is genuinely unsettled. **Dante, one of if not the oldest currently active
known non-Dreadnought Space Marines, is over 1,100 years old**; a former High Marshal of the
Black Templars, **Ludoldus**, **appears to have lived even longer, having served for over 2000
years**; and **Gravius**, a Salamander from the Great Crusade, was **stranded in space and not
rediscovered for some 10,000 years** — **still alive in an inactive state, though withered and
frail to the point of warranting being given a merciful death upon
rediscovery**.[^lex-space-marines]

The sources draw the careful conclusion rather than the exciting one. **This all lends to the
theory that Space Marines while subject to gradual aging are "functionally immortal", though
since they all inevitably die in battle one day there is not enough sufficient data to come to
any firm conclusions.**[^lex-space-marines]

Nobody knows whether a Space Marine can die of old age, because none has ever been given the
opportunity.

## Nineteen Organs, Give or Take {#implants}

The implant list is the most precisely documented piece of Imperial engineering in this codex,
and the first thing the sources say about it is that the count is uncertain.

**The 19–22 (plus, rarely, 1) implanted organs are very complicated, and because several of
them only work properly or at all in the presence of other implants, the removal, mutation, or
failure of one organ can affect the precise functioning of the
others.**[^lex-creation-of-a-space-marine] Because of that, **and the fact that each Chapter's
gene-seed belongs to that Chapter alone, different Chapters display different characteristics
and use different sets of implants and methods of
implantation.**[^lex-creation-of-a-space-marine]

There is no single specification. There are Chapter specifications, diverging, and the
Imperium cannot state its own total.

The organs, in the order a recruit receives them, do the following.

**The Secondary Heart** is the second heart, and the **Ossmodula** rebuilds the
skeleton — **a small, complex, tubular organ** whose **hormones affect the ossification of the
skeleton and encourage the forming bone growths to absorb ceramic-based chemicals laced into
the Marine's diet**.[^lex-creation-of-a-space-marine] The **Biscopea** drives muscle growth,
and the **Haemastamen** alters the blood.

Then a set that reads like a list of ways to make a body survive things bodies do not survive.
The **Larraman's Organ** clots wounds. The **Catalepsean Node**, **implanted into the back of
the brain**, **influences the circadian rhythms of sleep and the body's response to sleep
deprivation** — **if deprived of sleep, the catalepsean node cuts
in**.[^lex-creation-of-a-space-marine] The **Preomnor** is **essentially a pre-stomach that can
neutralise otherwise poisonous or indigestible foods**, acting as **a decontamination chamber
placed before the natural stomach**.[^lex-creation-of-a-space-marine] The **Multi-lung**
**activates when a Space Marine needs to breathe in low-oxygen or poisoned atmospheres** and
**even allows Space Marines to breathe water**.[^lex-creation-of-a-space-marine] The **Oolitic
Kidney**, with the second heart, **allows a Space Marine to filter his blood very quickly,
rendering him immune to most poisons** — at a price, since **this emergency detoxification
usually renders the Marine unconscious while his blood is circulated at high
speed**.[^lex-creation-of-a-space-marine]

The senses are rebuilt too. The **Occulobe** lets Apothecaries **make adjustments to the growth
patterns of the eye and the light-receptive retinal
cells**.[^lex-creation-of-a-space-marine] The **Lyman's Ear** takes the hearing. The
**Neuroglottis** **enhances a Space Marine's sense of taste to such a high degree that he can
identify many common harmful chemicals by taste**, extending to **tasting the air** — so that
**a Marine can even track a target like a hound by tasting or chewing on something, or even by
just sampling air**.[^lex-creation-of-a-space-marine]

Two are stranger than the rest and deserve stopping on.

The **Omophagea**, **also called "the Remembrancer"**, **allows a Space Marine to 'learn by
eating'**. **It is situated in the spinal cord but is actually part of the brain. Four nerve
bundles are implanted connecting the spine and the stomach wall. Able to 'read' or absorb
genetic material consumed by the marine, the omophagea transmits the gained information to the
Marine's brain.**[^lex-creation-of-a-space-marine]

A Space Marine can eat a creature and acquire its memories. That is a Tyranid capability —
[[VI.T4.Ch13]] records the Hive Mind absorbing biomass and learning from what it consumes — and
the Emperor built it into his soldiers deliberately, as a battlefield intelligence tool. The
Imperium that abhors the alien installed a xenos-like organ in its finest troops and gave it a
poet's name.

The **Sus-an Membrane** is **initially implanted above the brain** and **eventually merges with
the recipient's entire brain**; with training, a Marine can enter suspended
animation.[^lex-creation-of-a-space-marine] Gravius, above — alive after ten thousand years
adrift — is that organ working exactly as intended, for far longer than anyone designed it to.

The **Melanchromic Organ** **controls the amount of melanin in a Marine's melanin-possessing
organs (skin, hair, and eyes)**.[^lex-creation-of-a-space-marine] **Betcher's Gland**, in the
lip or hard palate, **works in a similar way to the poison gland of venomous reptiles**,
letting a Marine spit acid.[^lex-creation-of-a-space-marine]

And last, the one every reader has seen. The **Black Carapace** is **the most distinctive
implant**: **it resembles a film of black plastic that is implanted directly beneath the skin
of the Marine's torso in sheets**, hardening on the
outside.[^lex-creation-of-a-space-marine] It is the interface that makes power armour an
extension of the body rather than a suit worn over one, and it is the final stage of the
process.[^lex-space-marines]

## The Progenoid, and Why a Chapter Collects Its Dead {#progenoid}

One implant exists for a purpose that has nothing to do with the Marine carrying it.

**There are two of these glands, one situated in the neck and the other within the chest
cavity. These glands are vitally important and represent the future of the Chapter, as the only
way new gene-seed can be produced is by reproducing it within the bodies of the Marines
themselves. This is the implant's only purpose.**[^lex-creation-of-a-space-marine]

A Space Marine carries, in his throat and his chest, the organs from which his replacements
will be grown. He is not only a soldier. He is the Chapter's reproductive system, and the
sterility recorded in the first Section is what makes this the *only* route.

**Gene-seed is the unique genetic material used to transform a normal human into a super-human
Space Marine.**[^lex-gene-seed] It is **used to grow the artificial organs that distinguish an
Adeptus Astartes from an ordinary human, and helps alter the human's biology to accept the
implanted organs**. And then the sentence that explains a great deal of Space Marine
behaviour: **gene-seed is a Space Marine Chapter's most precious resource, and is conserved by
extracting the progenoid glands from fallen Battle-Brothers for return to the Chapter's
fortress-monastery.**[^lex-gene-seed]

This is why the Astartes retrieve their dead, and why an Apothecary's first duty on a
battlefield is not to the wounded. A body left behind is two glands lost, and two glands are
future Marines who will now never exist. [[III.T4.Ch20]] records Abaddon's raiders taking Blood
Angels gene-seed in the Black Crusades; this is what they were stealing — not a sample, but a
Chapter's capacity to replace itself.

The material is not uniform and does not stay stable. **Several Chapters have unique gene-seed
flaws that cause differences in their Marines' physiology and behaviour. These differences can
be merely cosmetic (for instance, all Death Spectres are albinos), while others can have
drastic effects that are either a blessing or a curse (or both) for their Chapters, such as
the Blood Angels or the Space Wolves.**[^lex-gene-seed]

**Some Chapters' gene-seed are inherently more stable than others, and variations in certain
Chapters' methods of implantation have resulted in further degradation in some
cases.**[^lex-gene-seed] One line settles a question that runs through the rest of this Title:
**the gene-seed of the Ultramarines is considered the most stable and thus they are the most
preferred source**.[^lex-gene-seed]

That is why so many Chapters descend from one Legion, and [[IV.T4.Ch15]] follows what the
Foundings did with it. The Imperium's answer to genetic decay was to keep going back to the
same well.

## The Primaris Implants {#primaris}

Three organs postdate everything above by ten thousand years, and they were built by the man
[[III.T5.Ch24]] records emerging from Mars with an army nobody knew existed.

They are inserted between the third and fourth phases of the ordinary sequence, **their ideal
age is 12–13**, and they are numbered separately as **the Primaris Alpha and Primaris Beta
phases**.[^lex-creation-of-a-space-marine]

The **Sinew Coils**, also called **the Steel Within**: **the Space Marine's sinews (tendons and
ligaments) are reinforced with durametallic coil-cables that contract with incredible force,
dramatically magnifying the subject's strength**.[^lex-creation-of-a-space-marine]

The **Magnificat**, **the Amplifier**: **a small thumbnail-sized lobe inserted into the brain's
core**, which **secretes hormones that increase the body's growth functions while also
intensifying its advanced systems**.[^lex-creation-of-a-space-marine]

And the **Belisarian Furnace**, **the Revitalizer** — **a dormant organ connecting to both
hearts** which, **in times of extreme stress or trauma, expels self-manufactured chemicals
similar to combat stimms that also aid in
regeneration**.[^lex-creation-of-a-space-marine]

An organ named after Belisarius Cawl, in the bodies of the soldiers he built. [[III.T5.Ch24]]
records the Primaris as ten thousand years of work carried out on Guilliman's order and
completed without his knowledge; this is the physical evidence of it, and it is worth noting
what it means. Cawl succeeded in adding to the Emperor's design. The Mechanicus of
[[IV.T6.Ch19]] holds innovation to be heresy and cannot build a machine it lacks a template
for, per [[III.T2.Ch7]] — and one Archmagos spent ten millennia improving on the work of the
Emperor himself, then delivered it to a Primarch.

The result is measurable. **The strength and endurance of Primaris Space Marines is even
greater than those of Firstborn.**[^lex-space-marines]

Which leaves the Imperium with two grades of the same soldier walking the same battlefields,
one of them demonstrably better, both drawn from the same hive gangs by the same hunts, and
the difference between them entirely the work of a man who was told to wait and did not.
