# The 9 Loyalist First Founding Legions

## Nine of Twenty {#nine}

**The twenty Space Marine Legions, also known as the Legiones Astartes, were created by the
Emperor.**[^lex-space-marine-legion] Nine of them were still loyal when the Heresy ended. This
Chapter is those nine, and the first thing to say about them is that they no longer exist in
the form the name implies.

**The loyalist Legions were broken up by Roboute Guilliman as per the new Codex
Astartes**, and **later reorganised by the Codex Astartes and broken up into several 1,000
Marine-strong Chapters during the Second Founding**.[^lex-space-marine-legion]

[[IV.T4.Ch13]] gives the reasoning: a book written by a frightened government, capping any
Astartes force at a thousand men. So "the nine loyalist Legions" names nine bloodlines rather
than nine armies. Each survives as a First Founding Chapter keeping the original name, plus
successors carrying the same gene-seed — and [[IV.T4.Ch15]] follows what became of them.

Two things recur across all nine, and both are worth having before the individual entries.

Every Legion has a Roman numeral, and the numerals are not consecutive among the loyal. I, V,
VI, VII, IX, X, XIII, XVIII, XIX survived; the gaps are the traitors of [[III.T3.Ch14]] and the
two Legions the Imperium does not discuss. And every Legion had an earlier name, from before
its Primarch was found — the Emperor raised these forces on Terra and named them again as the
scattered sons of [[III.T3.Ch12]] were recovered.

## Ultramarines, Imperial Fists, Blood Angels {#ultramarines-fists-angels}

The **Ultramarines**, **originally known as the War-Born**, were **the XIII Legion of the
original twenty**.[^lex-ultramarines] Their homeworld is **Macragge**, their Primarch **Roboute
Guilliman**, and their present Chapter Master **Marneus Calgar**; their battle cry is
**"Courage and Honour!"**[^lex-ultramarines]

They matter disproportionately for two reasons already established. [[IV.T4.Ch13]] records that
their Primarch wrote the Codex Astartes, and [[IV.T4.Ch12]] records that **the gene-seed of the
Ultramarines is considered the most stable and thus they are the most preferred source**. The
Legion that supplied the rulebook also supplies the raw material — which is why so much of
[[IV.T4.Ch15]] is a list of Chapters descended from this one. Their realm is treated in [[II.T2.Ch6]] —
the five hundred worlds that pay no tithe.

The **Imperial Fists** were **the VII Legion of the twenty original Space Marine Legions, the
sons of the Primarch Rogal Dorn**. They were **one of the nine legions which remained loyal
during the Horus Heresy, after which it was reorganised according to the Codex
Astartes**.[^lex-imperial-fists] Their homeworld is **Terra, formerly Inwit**, their battle cry
**"Primarch-Progenitor, to your glory and the glory of Him on
Earth"**.[^lex-imperial-fists]

A Chapter whose homeworld is Terra itself. Dorn fortified the Imperial Palace for the Siege of
[[III.T3.Ch16]], and his sons never left. [[IV.T1.Ch1]] records the one occasion the Astartes
exclusion from the Senatorum was broken — during the War of the Beast, when two successive
Imperial Fists Chapter Masters, Koorland and Maximus Thane, held the chairmanship of the
Imperium because the civilian government had stopped working.

The **Blood Angels**, **originally known as the Revenant Legion**, were **the IX Legion under
their Primarch Sanguinius**. They are **particularly well known for their bloodthirsty nature
in battle**.[^lex-blood-angels] Their homeworld is **Baal** and their Chapter Master is
**Dante** — the Marine [[IV.T4.Ch12]] records as **over 1,100 years old**, the oldest active
Astartes known.

Their defining trait is a wound rather than a temperament. **The death of Sanguinius caused the
Black Rage to suddenly fall upon the Blood Angels, both on the *Vengeful Spirit* and on
Terra.**[^lex-blood-angels] Their Primarch died at the Siege, and ten thousand years later his
sons still experience his death.

And it is getting worse. **In the countless generations since the Heresy, genetic
deteriorations in the Blood Angels gene-seed have seen an increase in this
flaw.**[^lex-blood-angels] [[IV.T4.Ch12]] records that gene-seed degrades and that Chapters
differ in stability; this is what that looks like from inside one of them. There is a
recorded hope attached to the newest arrivals: **Brother Corbulo becomes fascinated with the
Primaris Marines as a potential source for a cure to the Blood Angels
flaw.**[^lex-blood-angels]

Cawl's work of [[III.T5.Ch24]] may or may not fix a ten-thousand-year-old genetic injury. A
Chapter apothecary is following Primaris squads into battle to find out.

## Dark Angels, Space Wolves, White Scars {#angels-wolves-scars}

The **Dark Angels**, **known in High Gothic as Angelis Tenebraium, and originally known as the
Uncrowned Princes and the Angels of Death**, were **the I Legion of the twenty
original**.[^lex-dark-angels] The First. Their Primarch is **Lion El'Jonson**, their Chapter
Master **Azrael**, their homeworld **Caliban — destroyed** — leaving them
**fleet-based**.[^lex-dark-angels] Their battle cry is **"Repent! For tomorrow you
die!"**[^lex-dark-angels]

Their organisation carries a permanent secret duty. Their **Deathwing** are **Terminator
veterans and hunting the Fallen**, and **all of the Dark Angels descendants continue to work
together to hunt The Fallen as the Unforgiven**.[^lex-dark-angels]

The oldest Legion lost its homeworld and has spent ten millennia hunting its own. Every
successor Chapter of this bloodline is inducted into the same search, which makes it the only
bloodline in this Chapter organised around a fact about itself that the Imperium at large does
not know.

The **Space Wolves** — **known before the Horus Heresy as the Sky Warriors of Russ, the Rout or
Vlka Fenryka, Wolves of Fenris in the Fenrisian language** — were **the VI
Legion**.[^lex-space-wolves] Their Primarch is **Leman Russ**, their homeworld **Fenris**, their
Chapter Master **Logan Grimnar**, and their battle cry **"For Russ and the
Allfather"**.[^lex-space-wolves]

*The Allfather*, not the Emperor. [[II.T3.Ch7]] records that the Missionaria did not have to
convert Fenris — it only had to supply an Imperial name for a god the Fenrisians already had —
and [[IV.T3.Ch10]] records that as standard practice. The battle cry is the compromise,
preserved.

They are one of the two Chapters [[IV.T4.Ch13]] names as paying **mere lip service to the
Codex**, and their gene-seed is one of the two [[IV.T4.Ch12]] names as carrying drastic flaws.
One episode connects them to the present: **the 13th Great Company, led by Jorin Bloodhowl,
pursued the traitors and elements of the Wulfenkind would not be seen for the next 10,000
years.**[^lex-space-wolves]

A company of Space Marines went into the Eye of Terror after the Heresy and came back in the
41st Millennium.

The **White Scars**, **known to themselves as the Ordu of Jaghatai, or the Talskar, meaning
Sons of Chogoris, and originally known as the Star Hunters**, were **the V Legion of the
original twenty of the First Founding**.[^lex-white-scars] Their Primarch is **Jaghatai Khan**,
their homeworld **Chogoris**, their Chapter Master **Jubal Khan**, and their battle cry **"For
the Khan and the Emperor!"**[^lex-white-scars]

Three names for themselves and one imposed. The Imperium calls them White Scars; they call
themselves the Ordu, or the sons of their own world. [[I.T1.Ch2]] describes Low Gothic varying
world to world; this is a Chapter that kept its own language for its own identity and answers
to the Imperial name in Imperial company.

## Raven Guard, Salamanders, Iron Hands {#raven-salamanders-iron}

The last three are the Legions that were nearly destroyed, and each survived differently.

The **Raven Guard** were **the XIX Legion**. Their Primarch is **Corvus Corax**, and their
homeworld is **Deliverance, originally named Lycaeus, a moon which orbits the Hive World
Kiavahr**.[^lex-raven-guard] Their battle cry is **Victorus Aut Mortis** — **Victory or
Death**.[^lex-raven-guard]

A Chapter recruited from a prison moon above a hive world. [[IV.T4.Ch12]] establishes that the
Astartes prefer the lower levels of hives for their recruits; the Raven Guard's homeworld is
the penal colony that served one.

**The Legion was devastated at the outbreak of the Horus Heresy and survived only by the most
desperate of measures.**[^lex-raven-guard] Those measures were genetic, and they half-worked:
**within a few weeks more aspirants had been successfully implanted with stable gene-seed than
ever before**, the new material also meaning that **warriors matured into their new forms
rapidly compared to standard Astartes**.[^lex-raven-guard] And then the sabotage — traitors
**managed to covertly spike the gene tech with a daemon blood poison (while managing to keep
the pure technology for themselves), using the Raven Guard gene-seed to produce deformed
monsters**.[^lex-raven-guard]

A Legion facing extinction accelerated its own gene-seed to rebuild, and the acceleration was
poisoned. That is the same substance and the same logic as everything in [[IV.T4.Ch12]]: a
Chapter *is* its gene-seed, so the way to destroy one is to corrupt the material rather than
kill the men.

The **Salamanders**, **originally known as the Dragon Warriors**, were **the XVIII Legion**.
Their Primarch was **Vulkan**, their homeworld **Nocturne**, their Chapter Master **Tu'Shan**,
and their battle cry **"Into the fires of battle, unto the anvil of
war"**.[^lex-salamanders] **The Legion remained loyal during the Horus Heresy, but were
severely mauled**[^lex-salamanders] — and their recovery is recorded in a single concrete
image: **when Vulkan arrived he did not do so alone, he brought with him 3,000 new initiates
and equipment from Nocturne.**[^lex-salamanders]

The **Iron Hands** were **the X Legion, sometimes referred to as the "Iron
Tenth"**.[^lex-iron-hands] Their Primarch is **Ferrus Manus**, their homeworld **Medusa**, and
their battle cry is three words: **The Flesh Is Weak!**[^lex-iron-hands]

**The Iron Hands are notable for their heavy use of bionics and their reverence for all things
mechanical, and thus their close ties to the Adeptus Mechanicus.**[^lex-iron-hands] Their
Chapter Master, **Kardan Stronos**, is **elected by the Iron
Council**[^lex-iron-hands] — which makes them the only Chapter in this Chapter whose commander
is chosen by a vote rather than by succession or appointment.

A Legion that lost its Primarch at Isstvan and concluded that flesh was the problem. The
Mechanicus of [[IV.T6.Ch19]] holds that the machine is holier than the body, and one Space
Marine Chapter agrees with them doctrinally — which is why the Iron Hands sit closer to Mars
than to Terra, and why their battle cry is a theological statement rather than a boast.

Take the nine together and the pattern is one the Imperium never states. Two lost their
homeworlds. Two carry gene-seed flaws severe enough to define them. One was poisoned at the
root. One keeps a secret from the rest of the Imperium and inducts every descendant into
keeping it. Two pay lip service to the rulebook the Imperium regards as sacred, and one wrote
it. The nine loyal Legions are not nine versions of the same thing. They are nine survivors of
the same catastrophe, each damaged in a different place, and the Codex Astartes of
[[IV.T4.Ch13]] was written to make sure the damage could never again be pointed at Terra.
