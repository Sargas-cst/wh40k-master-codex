# Navis Imperialis Voidfleets

## Five Fleets, and Why the Navy Is Separate {#fleets}

The **Imperial Navy**, or **Navis Imperialis** in High Gothic, **is one of the armed forces
employed by the Imperium**. Where the Astra Militarum of [[IV.T5.Ch16]] holds the ground, **the
Imperial Navy is responsible for the fleets of warships that soar between the stars and
planets in the Imperium as well as engaging threats both inside and outside the Imperium's
borders**.[^lex-imperial-navy]

Its independence has a date and a cause. The Navy **is the military arm of the Imperial Fleet
and was, until its separation after the Horus Heresy, a branch of the Imperial
Army**.[^lex-imperial-navy]

During the Great Crusade it was **originally a subbranch of the Imperial Army known as the
Imperialis Armada**, and **during this time, the Imperialis Armada was primarily used for the
defence of Imperial space while planetary invasion was left to the fleets of the Legiones
Astartes**.[^lex-imperial-navy]

That division is the one the Heresy destroyed. When the Legions held the invasion fleets, half
of them defected with the means to cross the galaxy and land armies on any world. [[III.T4.Ch18]]
records the Legions being broken up afterwards; this is the naval half of the same reform. The
army no longer commands the ships, and the ships no longer belong to the Astartes.

The result is the arrangement [[IV.T5.Ch16]] describes from the other side: a Guard that cannot
go anywhere without a Navy that does not report to it, and a Navy whose foremost duty is to
carry an army it does not command. Neither can act without the other, which is precisely the
point.

Structurally the Navy maps onto the galaxy's largest divisions. It **is organised into five
fleets, each assigned to one of the Segmentae Majoris. Each fleet is named for the Segmentum
in which it is based, such as Battlefleet Solar.** Those **fleets are then subdivided into
smaller fleets patrolling individual sectors and subsectors, and are also generally named after
the area they safeguard. For example, Battlefleet Gothic protects the Gothic
Sector.**[^lex-imperial-navy]

The five Segmenta are treated in [[II.T1.Ch1]], and their fleet headquarters are named:
**Segmentum Solar — Mars**; **Segmentum Pacificus — Hydraphur**; **Segmentum
Obscurus — Cypra Mundi**.[^lex-imperial-navy] The Navy's **administrative centre is the Naval
Headquarters on Terra**.[^lex-imperial-navy]

Segmentum Solar's fleet is based at Mars rather than Terra. The forge world of [[II.T2.Ch5]]
builds the ships, and the fleet that defends the Imperium's core is headquartered where they
are made — which places the Mechanicus of [[IV.T6.Ch19]] between the Navy and its own
replacements.

## What a Battlefleet Is {#battlefleet}

**A Battlefleet comprises the vessels responsible for protecting a sector of the Imperium. A
battlefleet is commanded by a Lord Admiral and is the largest operational naval organisation,
usually consisting of between 50 and 75 ships of varying size. Depending on the importance or
enemy threat level of a sector this number may be more or
less.**[^lex-battlefleet]

Fifty to seventy-five ships to hold a sector — and [[II.T1.Ch1]] records that a sector may
contain hundreds of inhabited worlds. **There are thousands of sector battlefleets in the
Imperium.**[^lex-battlefleet]

The distribution is not even, and the exception is enormous. **Battlefleet Solar is home to
thousands of ancient, planet-killing battleships by itself.**[^lex-battlefleet]

One fleet holds thousands of capital ships while a typical sector fleet holds fifty. The
Imperium's naval strength is concentrated around Terra to a degree that leaves the rest of the
galaxy thinly covered, which is the same allocation [[III.T5.Ch26]] records going wrong: whole
systems in Segmentum Pacificus fell silent to the Tyranids and nobody noticed, partly because
the garrisons had been stripped for the Indomitus Crusade.

Composition is mixed by design. **A battlefleet is made up of destroyers, frigates, cruisers,
and battleships, as well as countless small vessels such as transports, shuttles, messenger
craft and long-range patrol craft**, plus **numerous non-warp travel ships such as system
patrol ships**.[^lex-battlefleet]

Ship classes are not uniform between yards. A *Lunar* class built above one world **may bear
little resemblance to a *Lunar* class constructed above Kar Duniash in Ultima Segmentum and
even less so to a *Lunar* class constructed among the vast shipyards of
Mars**.[^lex-imperial-navy]

Three ships of the same class, from three yards, that are not the same ship. [[III.T2.Ch7]]
records why: the Mechanicus works from templates it does not fully understand, and a pattern
copied for ten thousand years in three places diverges. The Imperium's warship classes are
naming conventions rather than specifications.

Their armament is what the void permits. Ships mount **broadside batteries, lance turrets and
starfighter hangars**[^lex-imperial-navy]; some carry **torpedoes and attack craft so that they
can get into position more quickly and safely**[^lex-imperial-navy]; and specialised hulls
exist, such as **torpedo boat destroyers or the *Firestorm* class frigate with its
armour-piercing prow-mounted lance**.[^lex-imperial-navy]

## Who Crews a Warship {#crew}

The Navy's ships are the largest objects the Imperium moves, and the crew figures are the part
of this Title that most resembles the hives of [[IV.T2.Ch4]].

A battleship carries **crews of between 25,000 to 3,000,000 or more depending on sources,
including large numbers of Imperial Navy armsmen to defend against enemy boarding
assaults**[^lex-imperial-navy]; a cruiser, **a crew complement of 1,000–100,000, depending on
sources**.[^lex-imperial-navy]

The sources disagree by two orders of magnitude and the article says so rather than choosing.
A warship of the Imperium is a city — and like the hive cities, most of its population will
never see the outside of it.

Command runs from the Lord Admirals down. **Below the Lord Admirals are various Sector and
Battlefleet commanders, then individual ship Captains and junior officers, and finally a myriad
array of Non-Commissioned Officers.**[^lex-imperial-navy] Discipline is imported from the same
department that supplies it to the Guard: **the Imperial Navy has a corps of Commissars that
oversee the morale and discipline of Naval personnel, known as the Fleet
Commissariat**[^lex-imperial-navy] — the Munitorum body of [[IV.T2.Ch6]], whose officers hold
the right and duty to execute for cowardice.

Internal security is a standing force. The Navy **operates Naval Security Battalions, also
known as Naval Armsmen**, **units of armed soldiers intended to put down mutinies, board enemy
ships, and protect Imperial Navy installations**, **typically equipped with black body armour
and shotguns**.[^lex-imperial-navy]

*Intended to put down mutinies* is listed first, before boarding the enemy.

No Imperial warship can sail on its own people. **Imperial Navy vessels require personnel from
other Imperial elements to function. These include Navigators from the Navis Nobilite for
navigation, Astropaths for interstellar communication, and Tech-Priests from the Adeptus
Mechanicus for higher maintenance.**[^lex-imperial-navy]

Three separate institutions, none of them the Navy's, without any one of which the ship is
blind, mute or broken. [[IV.T9.Ch30]] holds the Navigators, [[IV.T9.Ch29]] the Astropaths and
[[IV.T6.Ch20]] the Tech-Priests. The interdependence recorded at the top of this Chapter runs
all the way down to the individual hull.

And **one of the foremost duties of the Navy is to transport the Imperial Guard to warzones,
and as such, every Navy vessel, no matter how small, will sport some means of training deck to
accommodate them**.[^lex-imperial-navy]

At the bottom of the hierarchy is a category the Imperium does not euphemise. **The lowest
level of personnel in the Navy hierarchy are bondsmen, who are little more than slaves.
Alongside Servitors, these poor souls, organised into Press Gangs, undertake the most thankless
and dangerous tasks on a vessel.**[^lex-imperial-navy]

How they are acquired is stated in three ways, each worse than the last. **Bondsmen are
acquired by various means, such as rounding up criminals or unsavoury elements of Imperial
society or simply being born into their status aboard a vessel. Other times, if a ship is low
on such menials they may round up the nearest bystanders at random on the nearest
world.**[^lex-imperial-navy]

Criminals; people born into it aboard the ship; and anyone standing nearby when a ship is
short-handed. That last is not a punishment or a levy — it is abduction as a supply mechanism,
and it happens to whoever is closest to the landing site.

Their work and their conditions are recorded plainly. **Bondsmen's duties include cleaning,
hauling ammunition into weapons chambers, shovelling fuel, and manual labour. Working long
hours and only fed meagre rations, Bondsmen are met with brutal discipline should they protest
their status.**[^lex-imperial-navy]

Which completes this Title's account of how the Imperium mans its wars. The Guard is levied at
a tenth of every world's defenders and issued incomplete arsenals so it cannot rebel
effectively. The Navy is crewed at the bottom by people seized off the nearest street, kept in
line by armsmen whose first listed duty is suppressing mutiny and by Commissars who may shoot
them, and cannot move at all without borrowed Navigators, Astropaths and Tech-Priests. Neither
service can function alone, and both were arranged that way on purpose after the last time an
Imperial fleet and an Imperial army agreed with each other.
