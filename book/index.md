# Warhammer 40,000 Master Codex

An unofficial, independently written reference to the setting of Warhammer 40,000 —
its cosmology, geography, history, factions, wars, people and materiel.

It is built to be read two ways. Front to back it is a book, ordered so that nothing
important is referenced long before it is explained. Dipped into, it is a reference
work: any section can be read alone.

!!! warning "This codex is not yet written"

    The structure below is fixed and the toolchain is in place, but almost no content
    exists yet. Nothing is drafted until it has been researched, and research has not
    begun. Pages that do not exist are not hidden — they simply are not here.

## What makes this different

Most fan-written lore is unsourced, and confident prose is indistinguishable from
invention once it is a few clicks deep. This codex is built the other way round.

- **Every load-bearing fact is traced to a source that was actually retrieved.** Names,
  dates, numbers, sequences and spellings all carry a citation. See the
  [Bibliography](appendix/bibliography.md).
- **What could not be verified is said to be unverified**, not smoothed over. Nothing is
  padded, and no gap is bridged with a plausible-sounding detail.
- **Where sources genuinely conflict, the text says so.** Warhammer 40,000 contradicts
  itself deliberately, and a reference work should record that rather than quietly pick
  a winner. Every conflict found is logged in the
  [Disputed Facts Register](appendix/disputed-facts.md).
- **Each subject is treated in full in exactly one place** and cross-referenced from
  everywhere else, so there is one authoritative account rather than five drifting ones.
  The [Master Index](appendix/master-index.md) says where each subject lives.

## An honest limit, stated up front

Codices, rulebooks and Black Library novels exist only in print and were not consulted.
Where a wiki cites a printed page, that citation is reproduced and **labelled as
unverified** — it records what the wiki claims, not something this codex has checked.

The distinction is kept everywhere, because collapsing it is how unsourced lore starts
looking authoritative. This codex can confirm what a wiki claims. It cannot confirm
whether the wiki is right, and it never pretends otherwise.
