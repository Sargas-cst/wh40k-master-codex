How this is built

// Sourcing protocol

TIER 1
Official Games Workshop web pages. Authoritative where reachable.
TIER 2
Lexicanum. The working ceiling of this project.
TIER 3
Fandom wiki. Leads only — corroborated at tier 2 before use.
NEVER
Reddit, forums, fan essays. Not sources.

Printed codices, rulebooks and Black Library novels are the authoritative texts for this setting, and they exist only in print. They were not consulted. Where a wiki cites a printed page, that citation is reproduced and labelled unverified by us — because nobody here opened that book. This codex can confirm what a wiki claims. It cannot confirm whether the wiki is right, and it never pretends otherwise.

What makes it different

Traced

Names, dates, numbers, sequences and spellings each carry a citation to a source that was actually retrieved, with the date it was read.

Contradicted

The setting contradicts itself deliberately. Every conflict found is logged in the register rather than silently resolved in favour of one account.

Unfinished

What could not be sourced is marked unverified, or cut. Nothing is padded and no gap is bridged with a plausible-sounding invention.
