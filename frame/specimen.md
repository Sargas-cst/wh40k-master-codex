## The Emperor's Beacon {#emperors-beacon}

The Astronomican is a psychic beacon on Terra whose light allows Navigators to
steer Imperial vessels through the warp.[^lex-astronomican] Its practical reach is
about fifty thousand light years across a galaxy roughly eighty-five thousand light
years wide — an asymmetry that does more to define the Imperium's borders than any
treaty or campaign, because Terra sits in the galactic west and warp travel beyond
the beacon's reach is severely constrained.[^lex-astronomican]

It is worth being precise about what the Emperor does here, because the popular
account overstates it. He does not supply the beacon's energy. That comes from a
standing choir of ten thousand psykers, of whom up to one hundred die each day; what
the Emperor supplies is the capacity to handle and aim a psychic output nothing else
could survive directing.[^lex-astronomican] The distinction matters for the
[Navis Nobilite](#){ .cx-xref } and for anyone assessing how fragile Imperial
astrogation actually is. The beacon is not a miracle sustained by a god. It is an
industrial process with a body count, and the bodies are the point.

[Warp travel](#){ .cx-term title="Faster-than-light transit achieved by entering the Immaterium, a parallel dimension of psychic energy, and re-entering realspace at a distant point." }
depends on it absolutely, which is why the beacon's failure is treated as a
civilisational rather than a logistical event.

## The Psyker Toll and its Disputed Cost {#psyker-toll}

The figures attached to the Astronomican are frequently reported as a contradiction,
and they are not one. A standing population of ten thousand and a loss of up to a
hundred a day are not competing claims about the same quantity; they are a pool and a
rate of attrition from it.[^lex-astronomican] Both carry the same citation marker in
the source, resolving to a single page of a single book — which is the detail that
settles it, and precisely the detail a search summary destroys when it strips the
markers that show two figures share a source.

A separate figure, of a thousand sacrificed daily, is often folded into the same
discussion. It does not appear in this material at all, and belongs to a different
subject entirely.

!!! disputed "Recorded conflict — imperial-year-fraction"

    The fraction of an Imperial year represented by a check number is given
    variously as roughly eight and a half hours and as eight hours forty-five
    minutes, while arithmetic on a standard year yields about eight hours
    forty-six. This is logged in the
    [Disputed Facts Register](#){ .cx-xref } rather than resolved, because no
    source supports choosing between them.

!!! undefined-term "No source defines this — the-veil-as-structure"

    "The Veil" is in wide use for the boundary between the Materium and the
    Immaterium, but no retrieved source defines it as a structure with measurable
    properties. It is treated here as a metaphor, and deliberately given no
    thickness, composition or location. The absence is the finding.

!!! unverified "Could not be confirmed"

    Whether the Emperor's direction of the beacon is continuous or intermittent.
    The retrieved article does not say, and nothing else consulted addresses it.

Everything above rests on a single tier 2 article. That is a thin foundation for a
