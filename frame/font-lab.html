The Astronomican
The Emperor's Beacon

The Astronomican is a psychic beacon on Terra whose light allows Navigators to steer Imperial vessels through the warp.1 Its practical reach is about fifty thousand light years across a galaxy roughly eighty-five thousand light years wide.1

He does not supply the beacon's energy. That comes from a standing choir of ten thousand psykers, of whom up to one hundred die each day. The beacon is not a miracle sustained by a god. It is an industrial process with a body count.
